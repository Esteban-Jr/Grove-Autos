About Grove Autos

A Family Tradition
of Excellence

As a family-run accident and bodywork repair centre, we are dedicated to providing a very high quality of workmanship, excellent customer service and value for money. It's this ethos that has earned us a fantastic reputation throughout London over the past 20 years.

From small scratches to multiple panel repairs, and restoration projects from Land Rovers to Aston Martins — if you have any questions or enquiries, please don't hesitate to get in touch.

What We Do

Our Services

From minor scratches to full restorations — we handle it all with the same level of care and precision.

Accident Repairs

We repair any damage, from small scratches and dents to multiple panel repairs, including work carried out as part of an insurance claim.

Re-Sprays

Full and partial re-sprays in our spray booth, colour-matched to your vehicle — for cars and motorbikes.

Wheel Refurbishment

Alloy wheel repairs and refurbishment, and repainting in any colour you like.

Restoration

We have carried out a number of restoration projects over the years, from Land Rovers to Aston Martins.

Diagnostics

Vehicle diagnostics to find and fix warning lights, electrical and mechanical faults.

Air Conditioning

Air conditioning recharging to keep your car cool in summer and your windows clear in winter.

Servicing

Regular servicing to keep your vehicle running smoothly and reliably.
